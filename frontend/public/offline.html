⚡ Electrical Hazard Emergency Protocol

  • Switch OFF the main distribution board or meter switch immediately.
  • DO NOT touch any person in contact with live wire with bare hands. Use dry wood.
  • NEVER spray water on an electrical flame or smoking outlet.

💧 Major Water Rupture / Flooding Protocol

  • Shut the main gate valve below the overhead tank or municipal inlet.
  • Turn off electric circuit breakers in the wet room to avoid lethal conduction.
  • Elevate appliances and power strips above floor level.

🚗 Roadside Vehicle Breakdown Protocol

  • Move the vehicle fully onto the outer road shoulder away from active traffic.
  • Turn on hazard flashers immediately.
  • Stand behind the highway crash barrier while waiting for assistance.
